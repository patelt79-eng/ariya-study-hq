## History (`history.ts`)
- **Silk Road**:
  - "about 6,400 km"
  - grew "from around 130 BC" under the Han dynasty
  - named "Silk Road" in 1877 by a German geographer (Ferdinand von Richthofen)
  - Black Death spread in the 1340s

  - Merv "one of the largest cities in the world at times"
  - Marco Polo left Venice in 1271
- **Baghdad**:
  - founded 762 by al-Mansur on the Tigris; Round City with four gates
  - House of Wisdom at its peak under al-Ma'mun (813–833)
  - al-Khwarizmi and algebra/"algorithm"
  - al-Razi and smallpox/measles
  - paper made from the 790s
  - Zubaidah
  - Mongols 1258
  - The women's-rights summary is simplified.
- **Anglo-Saxons**:
  - hierarchy: king, thegns, ceorls, slaves

  - Hadrian's Wall begun AD 122; left c. 410
  - City names: Londinium, Eboracum, Aquae Sulis, Deva, Verulamium, Camulodunum
